## Recommendation

**Use Trelis Hinglish with one fixed prompt (`hi` + `<|mixedcode|>`) for every clip, and convert it
for ticket 03.** It is the most accurate config and never translates, but it costs English accuracy
and speed. Both are stated below rather than rounded away.

**What it wins**
- Overall 11.0% vs 27.1% for the app today (turbo-auto). No clip was translated, vs 5 for turbo-auto.
- Hindi: 48.8% to 11.6% strict against Devanagari references, so the gain is not an artefact of the
  lenient metric. Hinglish: 52.5% to 15.2% (agnostic; there is no Devanagari reference for mixed speech).
- One fixed prompt is as good as telling the model the true language (`trelis-oracle`, 11.4%) and
  better than letting it detect (`trelis-auto`, 14.7%), so the app needs no language detection.
  Whether the language picker from ticket 01 stays is for ticket 03 to decide.

**What it costs**
- **English gets worse: 11.9% strict vs 6.6% for turbo, nearly twice the errors.** Telling it the
  language is English recovers only part of that (10.6%). Whether this is acceptable is a product call;
  for a Hinglish-first user the Hindi gain dominates, for an English-only user turbo stays better
  (Parakeet/turbo as an English-only option is already in the SPEC).
- **Speed:** Trelis is large-v3 (32 decoder layers vs turbo's 4) and ran about 1.8x slower than turbo
  here. Ticket 03 must confirm the Core ML conversion meets 1.5 s on an M1. Untested fallbacks to
  evaluate if it doesn't: a quantised Trelis conversion, or Deepro (faster, but 23.1% and translates 3 clips).

**Caveats**
- Small sample: 43 pure-Hindi reference words over 4 clips, 158 Hinglish, 227 English. Treat Hindi
  differences under ~5 points as noise.
- The mixedcode prompt writes English loanwords in Latin ("feature"), which a Devanagari reference
  counts as errors; that is why `trelis-auto` looks better on the Devanagari-reference Hindi column.
- turbo-hi's p95 of 13.28 s is hallucination: forced Hindi on English clips loops on output like
  "समय समय समय" until the token limit.
- Latency comes from MLX/PyTorch, not the app's Core ML, so compare rows with each other only.
- No config fixes product names (Supabase, Qwen, WhisperKit) or "invoice" / "finance team" (clip 8):
  that is the custom dictionary (ticket 08). Strict WER stays around 44% for Trelis because Hindi comes
  out in Devanagari: that is cleanup (ticket 05). The optional cleanup step in the scorer arrives with
  ticket 05 too.

## Addendum (ticket 03): Trelis is too slow in the app; switch to Oriserve + turbo

In-app measurements on an M1 Pro (Swift EvalRunner: silence trim + WhisperKit):

| Config | Overall WER (agnostic) | Overall WER (strict) | en | hi | hinglish | Median s | Translated |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| app-turbo (auto-detect) | 35.3 | 51.9 | 7.0 | 39.5 | 74.7 | 1.00 | 10, 19, 22, 26, 28 |
| app-trelis (hi + mixedcode, GPU) | 11.9 | 44.4 | 10.1 | 4.7 | 16.5 | 7.86 | none |

- Trelis matches its bake-off accuracy in the app, but the full-precision large-v3 never finished
  compiling for the Neural Engine (80+ min; Argmax itself excludes fp16 large-v3 on M1), and on the
  GPU its 32-layer decoder takes 7.9 s per clip against a 1.5 s target.
- Oriserve Hinglish Apex (`Oriserve/Whisper-Hindi2Hinglish-Apex` @ `f3214eed20b4`, Apache-2.0) is
  turbo-sized and writes Roman Hinglish directly:

| Config | Overall WER (agnostic) | Overall WER (strict) | en | hi | hinglish | Median s | Translated |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| oriserve-auto | 17.5 | **22.0** | 18.1 | 7.0 | 19.6 | 1.79 | none |
| oriserve-en | 17.5 | **22.0** | 18.1 | 7.0 | 19.6 | 1.80 | none |
| oriserve-hi | 121.5 | 125.7 | 213.2 | 4.7 | 21.5 | 1.81 | 6, 8, 27 |

**Decision (user):** a Hinglish / English picker. **Hinglish mode = Oriserve** with `<|en|>` forced
(identical to auto-detect, deterministic; `<|hi|>` wrecks English). **English mode = turbo** (best
English, 7.0%), because English must be near-perfect and Oriserve's English is 18.1% with real
errors beyond names ("LATMC", "WhatsApp" for Vercel). Turbo's remaining English errors are mostly
names and jargon, which the dictionary (ticket 08) targets. Auto-routing between the two is a roadmap
item; turbo's own language detection can't do it (it labels Hinglish clips 10, 16, 19, 26 as English).

- Accuracy matches the PyTorch bake-off (17.5 / 22.0), so the conversion is sound.
- On the GPU the encoder's fixed 30 s window costs ~2 s per clip; on the Neural Engine 12 s of speech
  takes 1.27 s and 20 s takes 1.5 s. The ANE needs a one-time compile on first load: **967 s (16 min)**
  on an M1 Pro, cached afterwards. Onboarding (ticket 07) must show it as progress.
- Converting needs the CPU-only test settings in `convert-model.sh`: whisperkittools' default Neural
  Engine load tests took 60+ min and left `ANECompilerService` busy for over an hour after being stopped.
- Names are still wrong in Hinglish mode (Gurjeet → "gurujit"/"Gurjit", Supabase → "WhatsApp"), worse
  than turbo on Gurjeet; that is the dictionary's job (ticket 08).
